🎨 CUSTOMIZATION TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Want to change colors?

  1. EDIT globals.css
     Change color variables to your preference

  2. UPDATE PAGES
     Replace 'amber' with your color:
     • bg-amber-700 → bg-yourcolor-700
     • text-amber-950 → text-yourcolor-950
     • border-amber-200 → border-yourcolor-200

  3. ADJUST GRADIENT
     Edit background gradient in each page:
     from-amber-50 via-orange-50 to-amber-100

  4. CHANGE BUTTONS
     Update button colors globally
