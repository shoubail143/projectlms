## 💡 Customization Options

### Change Primary Color
Edit `globals.css` and replace `amber` with your color:
```css
--accent-warm: #yourcolor;
```

### Adjust Background Gradient
Edit any page and modify:
```jsx
bg-gradient-to-br from-amber-50 via-orange-50 to-amber-100
```

### Update Button Colors
Replace `bg-amber-700` with your preference in all pages

### Change Text Colors
Replace `text-amber-950` and `text-amber-700` globally
